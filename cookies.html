Cookie policy

Last updated October 11, 2026

Nexora sets no cookies. No advertising cookies, no analytics, no tracking.

What is kept in your browser

A few settings are kept in this tab’s session storage so the site works. They are essential, they never leave your device through Nexora, and they are erased when you close the tab.

  • nexora-band: your age group, to pick a look
  • nexora-lang: your language choice
  • nexora-privacy-notice: that you closed the cookie notice
  • nexora-consent: that a grown-up said yes (under-18s)
  • nexora-handoff: a question passed from the home page to the chat, removed straight away

Files from other companies

To load fonts, code libraries and the voice tool, your browser contacts Google Fonts, jsDelivr, cdnjs and, only when you join a voice classroom, Agora. They see your IP address. We do not ask them to set cookies, but see their policies for details.

Your choice

Because Nexora uses only essential storage, there are no optional cookies to accept or reject. If that ever changes, we will ask first, and saying no will be as easy as saying yes. You can clear this tab’s data any time on Delete my data.
