Nexora has no accounts, so most of what it holds lives only in your browser tab.

1. Clear this device now

2. Ask us to delete data

If you used a live classroom or chat and want any related data removed, email us. You do not need to give your real name. Tell us roughly when you used Nexora and your room code if you joined one. Parents and guardians can ask on behalf of a child.

We keep no user database, so we will tell you what we could delete and which provider holds anything else. We answer within 30 days.
