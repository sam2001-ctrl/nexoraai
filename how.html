Your privacy comes first.

Nexora doesn’t use advertising cookies or analytics. Chat stays in this tab and is sent to the selected AI provider only when you ask a question.

YOUR CHOICES

Privacy & cookies

Nexora does not set advertising, analytics, or tracking cookies. The site uses only temporary browser storage needed to remember that this notice was dismissed and which language you picked. It stores no chat history or learning goals between visits.

When you use chat or voice

Your message is sent to the configured AI provider to generate a reply. Live voice connects to Agora when you choose to join and allow microphone access. The host may keep standard security logs. Avoid entering sensitive or identifying information.

Your controls

You can dismiss this notice, clear this tab by closing it, and leave an Agora room at any time. Microphone permission can be revoked in your browser settings.
