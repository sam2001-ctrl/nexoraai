Privacy policy

Last updated October 11, 2026

Nexora is a free learning helper with a text chat and a live voice classroom. It has no accounts and no ads. This page explains what happens to the information you give it.

What we collect, and why

  • Your age. Typed on the first page and used only in your browser to choose a look. We keep just a label (under 13, 13–17 or 18+) in this tab. Your age is never sent to our server.
  • Settings. Your language choice, whether you closed the cookie notice, and whether a grown-up said yes. All of these stay in this tab.
  • Chat messages and your goal. Sent to our server and on to an AI service (OpenRouter, Groq or Google Gemini, depending on how Nexora is set up) so it can write a reply. Some questions, such as facts or weather, also trigger a lookup with public data services. We do not store your chat. It stays in this tab until you press Clear chat or close it.
  • Live classroom. Room code, nickname, role and microphone audio go to our server and to Agora, the real-time voice service, so the class can talk and Nexora can answer. Please use a nickname, not your full name.
  • Technical data. Your IP address is held in memory for a short time to stop abuse. It is not saved by us. Our web host and the services above may keep their own security logs.

What we do not do

We do not use accounts, advertising, analytics or tracking, we do not sell information, and we do not build profiles of students.

Children and teenagers

Anyone under 18 is asked to confirm that a parent, guardian or teacher is OK with them using chat or voice. This is a tick box, not a check of identity. Please don’t type personal details such as full name, address, phone number or school name. A parent or guardian can ask us to delete anything linked to their child at any time.

Who else handles your information

Google Fonts (fonts), jsDelivr and cdnjs (code libraries), Agora (voice), your chosen AI provider, and our web host. They receive your IP address and browser details when you load their files or use their service, and have their own policies. See licenses and services.

Keeping and deleting data

We keep no database of users or chats. Data in your tab disappears when you close it. To clear it now, or to ask us to delete something, use Delete my data.

Your rights

Depending on where you live you may be able to ask to see, correct or delete your information, or to withdraw permission. Write to privacy@your-domain.example and we will answer within 30 days.

Changes

If we change how Nexora handles information, we will update this page and ask again where permission is needed.

This policy is a template written for this project. Have it checked against the laws that apply to you before launch.
